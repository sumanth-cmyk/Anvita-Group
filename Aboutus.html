Our Team

Mr. Atchuta Rao Boppana

Managing Director, Anvita Group.

Under the dynamic leadership of Mr. Atchuta Rao Boppana, Anvita group expanded into new markets, including Hyderabad and the United States. Mr. Atchuta Rao Boppana, an accomplished civil engineer, embarked on his professional journey with the prestigious infrastructure giant Larsen & Toubro (L&T). His early career at L&T, renowned for its significant contributions to infrastructure development, laid a solid foundation for his expertise in engineering and project management. Building on this experience, Mr. Atchuta Rao Boppana expanded his horizons internationally, in Dubai, a burgeoning hub for global construction and real estate development.

In 2004, Mr. Atchuta Rao Boppana made a strategic transition from corporate employment to entrepreneurship. He leveraged his extensive industry knowledge and experience by forming partnerships with various companies within the real estate and construction sectors. Mr. Atchuta Rao Boppana's journey from a civil engineer at L&T to a successful entrepreneur with a global footprint underscores his vision, dedication, and adaptability in the ever-evolving field of real estate and construction.

Parkside: Hyderabad’s
New Icon of Indulgence

Anvita Parkside near ORR Exit 6, Medchal, is an eco-friendly community designed for a sustainable and holistic lifestyle. Spanning 50 acres with over 75% open spaces, this exclusive development features just 270 unique Villaments, each thoughtfully designed with a spacious 20-foot front yard and backyard. Emphasizing green living, every home is solar-powered, ensuring energy efficiency and environmental sustainability. Residents can enjoy a 2.4-acre resort-like clubhouse with abundant parking, offering a perfect blend of luxury and leisure. Anvita Parkside is more than just a residential space—it's a self-sustaining, health-centric haven that redefines modern living amidst nature.
